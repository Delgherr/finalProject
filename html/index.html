Top 5 Best-Selling Video Games of All Time

A list of the five best-selling video games.

  1. Mario Kart 8 + Deluxe – 70.43 million
  2. Wii Sports - 82.9 million
  3. Grand Theft Auto 5 - 200 million
  4. Minecraft - 300 million
  5. Tetris - 520 million

Mario Kart 8 + Deluxe – 70.43 million

Mario Kart 8 Deluxe is a fun racing game where players compete on colorful tracks using different characters and special items. It has sold more than 70 million copies, making it one of the best-selling games of all time.

Developer:Nintendo

Wii Sports - 82.9 million

Wii Sports includes five sports: tennis, baseball, bowling, golf, and boxing. Its motion controls made it a favorite for families and helped it sell over 82 million copies worldwide.

Developer:Nintendo

Grand Theft Auto 5 - 200 million

Grand Theft Auto V is an open-world action game that lets players explore the city of Los Santos, complete missions, and play online. It has sold more than 200 million copies worldwide.

Developer:Rockstar North

Minecraft - 300 million

Minecraft is a sandbox game where players can build, explore, and survive in a world made of blocks. With over 300 million copies sold, it is one of the most popular video games ever created.

Developer:mojang

Tetris - 520 million

Tetris is a classic puzzle game where players arrange falling blocks to complete horizontal lines. Its simple gameplay and timeless design have made it one of the best-selling video games of all time.

Developer:The Tetris Company
